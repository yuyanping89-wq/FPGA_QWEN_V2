The large BRAM consumers among the compute modules are M07 (68 tiles), M11 (56), M13 (92), M06 (20), M08 (24), and M14 (22). The total BRAM use remains below half of device capacity because the current M13 implementation keeps only a 4×64 FP32 Mid tile (1 KiB) on chip and streams weights/activations from PL DDR; it does not store the complete 64×4864 FP32 Mid matrix in BRAM.

M07 and M11 use four-row output scheduling and BRAM-backed FP32 accumulators. M13 also uses four-row scheduling and packed partial-sum storage. These changes reduce replicated arithmetic and LUTRAM/control networks. Their cost is extra weight passes for long prefill: M07/M11 and M13 process a 64-row prefill in sixteen four-row blocks. The eight-row M13 candidate is not in this integrated bitstream.

## Stage and timing interpretation

The top-level routed report is the authoritative per-IP breakdown. The post-place summary in utilization_placed_20261015.rpt agrees on the whole-design counts. Per-IP reports under vivado/reports/per_ip_synthesis are synthesis-stage reports for individual cells, not routed numbers.

The packaged 2026-10-15 routed timing report shows zero failing setup endpoints and zero failing hold endpoints in the reported path groups. The worst listed setup slack is +3.184 ns and the worst listed hold slack is +0.054 ns; another clock group reports +0.066 ns setup slack. The routed DRC summary lists 5,308 Warning/Advisory findings and no Error-severity entries; the bitstream was generated. See the full timing_summary_routed_20261015.rpt and drc_routed_20261015.rpt before using this design as a new timing baseline.

The same-stage optimization history is in history/utilization_hier_optimized_20261014.rpt and history/utilization_optimized_20261014.rpt. At Optimized stage, total LUT fell from 356,237 to 247,704; the Routed total of 232,975 is a later physical-optimization/report-stage result, so the difference between those values is not all attributable to HLS source changes.
